THE STAR AT THE CENTER

The Sun

Our star is a giant ball of hot plasma. Its gravity holds the solar system together, while its light and heat make life on Earth possible.

What about Pluto? Pluto is a fascinating dwarf planet in the Kuiper Belt beyond Neptune. It was reclassified as a dwarf planet in 2006, and is still an important member of our solar system.
The big picture

Space is bigger than you think.

The distances between planets are enormous. Astronomers use the astronomical unit (AU), the average distance between Earth and the Sun, to measure the solar system. One AU is about 150 million kilometres.

And beyond Neptune lies the Kuiper Belt, a region filled with icy bodies, dwarf planets and clues to the solar system's earliest days.
